About me

My name is Alan Roy, and I am currently a junior attending the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign studing. I am studying mechanical engineering with a minor in electrical engineering. Ever since I was a child, I have been enamored with robotics. It began when I went to my hometown’s fair, Salmon Days, and saw the high school robotics team’s demonstration.

I carried that passion through high school and college, where I kept exploring the field and creating amazing things. I have fallen in love with all things design and control theory. Which together satisfy my desire to create and to investigate complex math.
